# PokéZ Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 15, 2026
**Support server:** https://discord.gg/2EcvVCPP94

This Privacy Policy explains what information the PokéZ Discord bot ("PokéZ", "the Bot", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Bot you agree to this policy.

## 1. Information we collect

**Discord identifiers.** To run the game we store your Discord user ID, and for servers where the Bot is used, the server (guild) ID and the IDs of channels the Bot is configured to use.

**Game data.** Your Pokémon collection and their stats, your selected Pokémon, coins, shards, redeems, incense and items, market, auction and trade records, battle and Pokédex progress, and the settings you choose (for example server prefix, language and reminder or silence preferences).

**Messages and commands.** The Bot reads messages in channels it can see so that it can detect commands and Pokémon name guesses. We process this content only to respond to the command or guess. We do **not** store the content of general messages, and we do not read direct messages other than those you send to the Bot.

**Technical logs.** We may keep short-term logs of commands used, errors and timestamps to fix bugs, prevent abuse and keep the Bot running.

We do **not** collect your real name, email address, phone number, password, payment information or precise location.

## 2. How we use information

We use the information to:

- run the game and show your profile, collection and progress;
- process catches, trades, auctions, battles and rewards;
- remember server settings;
- detect and stop cheating, automation, exploits and abuse;
- fix bugs and improve the Bot;
- answer support requests and appeals.

We do **not** sell your information and we do **not** use it for advertising.

## 3. Sharing

We share information only in these cases:

- **Discord.** The Bot runs on Discord and is subject to Discord's own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
- **Service providers.** Our hosting and database providers store and process data on our behalf so the Bot can work.
- **Other users.** Some game information is visible to other users by design, such as your Discord username on trades, auctions, the market and leaderboards.
- **Legal and safety.** We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the Bot, its users or others from abuse.

## 4. Data retention

Game data is kept while the Bot is in use so your progress is saved. Technical logs are kept only for a short time. If you ask us to delete your data, or if your data is no longer needed, we will delete or anonymize it, except where we must keep a record to enforce a ban or prevent abuse.

## 5. Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to:

- tell you what data we hold about you;
- correct inaccurate data;
- delete your PokéZ data (this resets your progress permanently).

To make a request, contact us in our support server at https://discord.gg/2EcvVCPP94. We may need to confirm that the Discord account is yours. Server administrators can also remove the Bot from their server at any time, which stops it from processing activity there.

## 6. Children

PokéZ is intended for people who are old enough to use Discord (13 or older, or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age. If you believe a child has used the Bot, contact us and we will delete the data.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we store. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. International use

The Bot and its providers may process data in countries other than yours. By using the Bot you understand that your information may be transferred and stored there.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the support server, and continued use of the Bot means you accept the updated policy.

## 10. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: join our support server at https://discord.gg/2EcvVCPP94.
